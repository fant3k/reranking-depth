# TREC 2023 product search replication
Freeze before scoring. Use the organizers' 2023 test queries, official qrels and pinned TREC product titles. This provides a separately judged benchmark from the Amazon catalog ecosystem. Exclude queries seen in ESCI training, calibration, earlier held-out probes or WANDS after casefold and word-token normalization. Retain one topic per normalized query and at least 20 distinct explicitly judged items with nonempty titles. Rank only these judged items by the original title BM25 formula and product-ID tie break. Use linear nonnegative official relevance grades and the ideal top ten from the whole judged pool. Exclude negative/unjudged grades. Report exclusions and zero ideals. Use actual MiniLM batches of 15, 20 and 10 + optional 10, with the original frozen post router and threshold. No training or threshold selection.
Primary quality criterion is lower 95% paired query-bootstrap bound of fixed15 minus fixed20 > -0.01. Report fixed15 minus post and share sent deep. Timing, when reported, is warm per-request service time excluding retrieval on the machine measured; do not transfer the old queue latency numbers. Eligible pools and benchmark judgments do not represent production clicks or an entirely different retailer.
